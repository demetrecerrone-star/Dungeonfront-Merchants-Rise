## Important visual quality distinction

The present procedural sprites are a **mechanically usable prototype**. Their extra surface lines and gradients **do not yet reproduce** the premium painterly anime look of the 6-character art reference. A poster arranged into rectangles is NOT six sets of consistently registered alpha animation frames. Replacing the current sheets with rows cropped from a poster would break anatomy, movement, transparency, and combat facing.

To reach the approved standard, produce independently illustrated, consistently oriented, **transparent single-frame** sources for each class, frame, and action, preserving each character's identity across all frames. Better to finish one full class at final quality than to fake six inconsistent animations.

### Input for final painted art
Place production illustrations under:
```text
art-source/anime-v1/<lowercase-class>/<action>/000.png
art-source/anime-v1/<lowercase-class>/<action>/001.png
...
```
Valid frame sizes: **128 × 192, 256 × 384, or 512 × 768**, with transparent RGBA backgrounds and consistent dimensions for each action. Frames should be aligned to the same 2D foot baseline, face right by default, and have no cropped hands/feet/weapons/VFX. Each of six classes uses:
- Idle 8 frames
- Walk 10 frames
- Attack 12 frames
- Hurt 5 frames
- Death 12 frames
- Special 12 frames

**Total: 59 per class, 354 separate final illustrations** before compact atlas packing. VFX should be in an independent overlay when its footprint exceeds the 128-pixel cell.

Use `python3 tools/pack_anime_illustrated_frames.py --class Ranger` for the first finished 59-frame illustrated character. The validator outputs review-only assets under `art-previews/anime-painted-source`, **never** overwrites Android assets.

4. Render the same final character in the **shop, public dungeon and contract dungeon**; preserve enemy-facing for ranged and melee attacks, upright 0-HP retreat, last-boss portal behavior and contract/extraction claims.
5. Pass Node/gameplay QA, all Android emulator smoke checks (plus class-specific facing and animation captures), signed install-over preview APK and acceptance on user's Samsung Galaxy S23 FE Android 16.
